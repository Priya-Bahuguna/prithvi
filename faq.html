Frequently asked questions

Why is environmental protection important?

A healthy environment supports human beings, animals, plants and the ecosystems all of them depend on.

How can students help the environment?

Students can reduce waste, save water, avoid unnecessary plastic, and spread awareness among friends and family.

What is sustainability?

Sustainability means meeting present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs.

What is biodiversity, briefly?

The variety of living organisms in an ecosystem — more variety generally means a more resilient ecosystem.
