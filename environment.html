Environmental problems

Air pollution

Occurs when harmful substances enter the atmosphere and affect living organisms, often traced back to vehicle exhaust and industrial emissions.

Water pollution

Happens when harmful substances contaminate rivers, lakes, oceans and other water resources, frequently from untreated waste and runoff.

Deforestation

The large-scale removal of forests, which can disrupt biodiversity, soil stability and local and global climate patterns.

Plastic pollution

Plastic waste can persist in the environment for centuries and is easily mistaken for food by wildlife.

Quick tip

Carry a reusable water bottle instead of buying single-use plastic ones — a small swap that adds up over a year.

In their own words

Nature is our shared responsibility — a short, inline idea worth repeating.

Protecting the environment requires responsible choices from individuals, communities and societies.

One influential report on this subject is Our Common Future, often cited by policy researchers at the UN.
