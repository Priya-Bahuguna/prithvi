Indian culture and nature

Indian traditions have long included practices connected with rivers, forests, animals and sustainable living — many of them centuries older than the modern word "sustainability."

Traditional environmental practices

  • Respect for rivers
  • Protection of sacred groves and trees
  • Community water conservation
  • Traditional rainwater harvesting (stepwells, tanks)
  • Respect for biodiversity in local folklore
